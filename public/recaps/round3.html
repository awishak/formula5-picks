Round 3 -- Japanese Grand Prix

Suzuka Recap

The Happiest Picks on Earth

I'm so glad I invited my childhood friends Larry and Chris to play this game so they could absolutely destroy me and Kevin this week. Chris Fondacaro and Larry Noel of Magic Kingdom Racing submitted identical picks. Not similar. Identical. Same top pick, same four midfielders, same finishing order, same best finish guess, same pit stop guess down to the decimal.

Collusion? No, it's called strategy. They out-strategized us, and the result was the highest team score of the year at 119, a fairy tale ending that turned my Cal Aggie Racing into a pumpkin at midnight. They also became the first two players in F5 history to earn the finishing order bonus, which the entire league had gone 0-for-96 on before two guys from Resurrection elementary school casually nailed it on the same week. There was bibbidi-bobbidi-nothing Kevin and I could do about it.

Box Box... Box Box... OKAY STOP BOXING

Racing Bulls' first pit stop was 4.78 seconds, which certainly injected some surprise but took the BOX BOX line and The Needle entirely out of our hands. The dial maxes out at 4.0, so there was nothing anyone could have done. Zero out of 48 scored needle points.

Sorry to those who lost on this, but we did have a funny one. Joe Hanna and Zack Girgis of East Bay Racing, having been burned in previous weeks, went all in on team play with a max 4.0 and 4.0 selection. Rumor has it they hired outside consultants for $120K to arrive at that number. Didn't matter. The over hit. Hard. Every single OVER team won their BOX BOX matchup this week, all twelve of them. You could have taken a nap during that pit stop and woken up in time to see it was still going.

The Machine Learning What It's Like to Be Human

Haha! Take THAT, technology! The Formula5 Bot picked Charles Leclerc as its top driver instead of Antonelli, scored 15 from that slot instead of 25, and finished 30th. Feel what it's like to be P30! What an idiot! (Please don't hold this against me when the bots take over and enslave us in the salt mines.)

For the first time in the 2026 season, the Bot was not on the individual podium. After going 1st in Round 1 and 2nd in Round 2, it finally knows the sting of mediocrity. I'll take that as a win for humanity. But... yeah, the Bot still leads the cumulative standings at 149, seven points clear of me at 142. So it's beating all 47 of us. The celebration was brief.

Jet Lag Pays Off

TJ Donato continues a long and proud tradition of F5 players doing well while attending Formula 1 events in person. He flew to Suzuka for the Japanese Grand Prix, probably ate a 7-Eleven egg salad sandwich at 6 AM because that's what you do in Japan, and turned in his best F5 result of the season: 7th overall with 55 points.

He picked Antonelli as top driver, loaded up on Piastri, Sainz, Gasly, and Lawson for 26 midfield points, and matched the best midfield haul in the league this week. We're not saying you have to fly 5,000 miles to do well in Formula 5, but the data is the data.

Luxor? More Like Lucks-or

See what I did there? It's because they're lucky. Pavly and Chris, with the second-lowest average team score in the entire league at 60.0, are somehow 3-0. They are one of two undefeated teams in all of Formula 5, alongside Van City Corsa.

This week they won with 57 total points, which would have lost to literally every other winning team. But they didn't play every other winning team. They played TNT Roku, who scored 46. These are the same guys who needed weekly text reminders to submit their picks last year. Now they're the 2006 Cardinals of Formula 5: nobody knows how, nobody knows why, but the record says 3-0 and the record doesn't lie.

Matchup Recaps
El Camino Rapido 91, East Bay Racing 91
Championship DivisionMatchup of the Week

The second tie in F5 history, and this one was entirely manufactured by a Racing Bulls mechanic having an off day. East Bay had 92 base points to El Camino's 86. Without BOX BOX, Joe Hanna and Zack Girgis walk away with a clean win. But the 4.78-second stop blew past the 3.02 line, El Camino got +5 as OVER, East Bay took -1 as UNDER, and a 6-point East Bay advantage became a dead heat.

Hanna posted 48 and Girgis had his best week at 44, and none of it mattered because physics decided that a wheel gun in Suzuka should take an extra two seconds. El Camino's Harold Gutmann (48) and Paul Kohli (38) will happily take it. El Camino (27 pts, 5th in Championship) stays alive. East Bay (11 pts, 12th in Championship) is the only Championship team without a win, and that Wednesday strategy lunch is clearly not working.

Magic Kingdom Racing 119, Cal Aggie Racing 96
Second Division

When you wish upon a star, apparently the star gives you the exact same picks as your teammate. MKR's 119 is the highest team score in the three-week history of Formula 5, and it would have beaten every single other team this week. Fondacaro (57 base + 10 weekly) and Noel (57 base + 9 weekly) were so in sync they might as well have been the same person.

Kevin Coolidge put up a solid 50 for Cal Aggie (good enough for 10th overall) and I added 48, but this was like bringing a dental pick to a Disney-themed cannon fight. MKR (27 pts, 5th in Second Division) gets their first win and it's a statement. Cal Aggie (18 pts, 11th in Second Division) will be hearing about this for a while.

Bronco SCUderia 83, Juicero Silicon Valley 81
Championship DivisionBOX BOX Decided

Juicero had the better roster this week. Kerolos Nakhla doing Kerolos Nakhla things at 48, George Fahmy adding 34 (though picking Hamilton as top driver for 8 points is, as they say, not the juice). Combined base: 82 to Bronco's 78. But Bronco was OVER and Juicero was UNDER, and 4.78 seconds of pit stop later, the 4-point Juicero lead became a 2-point Bronco win.

Maggie Mudge led Bronco with 44, Alicia Cho added 34 after a rough Round 2. Without the OVER sweep, Juicero wins this comfortably. With it, Bronco steals one. Bronco (18 pts, 11th in Championship) is quietly in the mix. Juicero (34 pts, 2nd in Championship) can take it up with Racing Bulls.

Scuderia Iskandaraya 89, Peloton Aubergine 84
Second DivisionBOX BOX Decided

Anthony Zamary had his best week of the season at 3rd overall with 62 points, nailing the best finish bonus when Antonelli won. Ramy Stephanos added 30. On the Peloton side, Stacy Michaelsen was quietly excellent at 60 (4th overall, her best result this year, 5th in the cumulative standings at 130), but Brett Dillon's 32 and the -1 BOX BOX left her stranded.

Stacy is doing everything right individually, but she can't carry the team alone. Without the BOX BOX swing, Peloton wins this one. Scuderia (22 pts, 9th in Second Division) takes the Moussaka Matchup and the season series lead. Peloton (28 pts, 4th in Second Division) needs Brett on the bike.

Garra Dynamics 106, Cougar Autosport 75
Championship Division

The freshmen woke up in a big way. Ryan Kohli went from dead last (48th) in Round 2 to 6th this week with 56, Francisco Soldavini finished 8th with 53, and their combined 101 base before BOX BOX is the second-highest base total of the season. Soldavini earned a Championship exemption for his real-life racing experience, and after two Garra players land in the top 8 in the same week, it's looking like that exemption was well deserved.

On the Cougar side, Lucia Thompson picked Hamilton as top driver and scored 31, which left Heather Ishak's 45 completely stranded in a 31-point blowout, the biggest margin of the week. Garra (25 pts, 6th in Championship) announces themselves as a team people need to take seriously, and Cougar (31 pts, 3rd in Championship) needs Lucia to put down the Hamilton jersey.

Luxor Motorsport 57, TNT Roku F5 Team 46
Second Division

The lowest-scoring matchup of the week, maybe the season, and Luxor still won it. Pavly Attalah posted 20 points. Twenty. Chris Malek added 32. And with the +5 BOX BOX they scraped together 57, which was more than enough because Andy Thompson scored 15, the worst individual result of the week.

Luxor (26 pts, 7th in Second Division) is winning games the way you win a limbo contest: by being lower than everyone else and somehow not falling over. TNT Roku (27 pts, 6th in Second Division) needs to find a floor, because right now there isn't one.

Stalloni 1851 94, Cascadia Motorsport 86
Championship Division

The Cal Poly Mustangs connection finally pays off as Stalloni gets their first win of the season. Schertler led with 48 and Civitts added 41, and with the +5 BOX BOX they were comfortable at 94 despite the Nobar brothers both putting up strong individual numbers (45 and 42) on the other side. Stalloni (23 pts, 7th in Championship) gets on the board while Cascadia (20 pts, 8th in Championship) has now dropped two straight after winning their opener.

Van City Corsa 91, Wildcat Motors 77
Championship Division

Van City Corsa is the class of the Championship Division at 3-0 and they did it this week despite being on the wrong side of BOX BOX, which tells you how good their roster is. When you have Zarifa (48) and Yousef (44) putting up those kinds of numbers, you don't need a slow pit stop to bail you out. Aditya Satish's 27 dragged Wildcat down, and what was a promising 2-0 start now comes with a first loss. Van City (47 pts, 1st in Championship) rolls on. Wildcat (18 pts, 10th in Championship) will be fine.

Prestissimo Veloce 86, Shoey Time! w/ Max and Danny 67
Second Division

Prestissimo gets their first win of the season and it's largely thanks to Matilda Luton, who picked Leclerc as top driver, posted 43 with a best finish bonus, and carried a Prestissimo team that now has the most balanced record in the league at 1-1-1: a win, a loss, and a tie through three weeks. Evie added 38, which was more than enough since Danny Bowers' 26 dragged Shoey Time down to a total that never had a chance. Prestissimo (21 pts, 10th in Second Division) keeps climbing. Shoey Time (34 pts, 2nd in Second Division) takes their first loss, and those champagne-filled racing boots might need a refill.

TJ Premium 91, Aggie Slipstream 64
Second Division

TJ came back from Suzuka and dropped 51 base points, the Bot added 41, and together they rolled Aggie Slipstream 91-64 in what was never a contest. Brian Dong picked Leclerc as top and scored 20 total, continuing his arc from Round 2 contrarian hero (the only player who skipped Norris) to Round 3 contrarian casualty. Aggie Slipstream (4 pts, 12th in Second Division) is the only winless team in Formula 5, and at some point the ASS acronym stops being intentional and starts being descriptive. TJ Premium (24 pts, 8th in Second Division) is trending up.

HomeworkTubes.Com 103, Meatballs 73
Second Division

Grant Wong finished 5th overall with 57 points and Sam Bottoms added 47, giving the Tube Men a 98 base before the +5 BOX BOX pushed them over 100 for the first time this season. Somewhere in the partially underwater Bahamas facility, Tubey the Worm (brought to you by Halliburton) is shedding a single proud tear, because HomeworkTubes.Com (30 pts, 3rd in Second Division) looks like a legitimate team now. Meatballs' Carnesecca (42) and Ball (32) couldn't keep up with the tubes this week and take their first loss. Meatballs (42 pts, 1st in Second Division) will bounce back.

Drivetex 101, XLIX Racing Team 81
Championship Division

The two UT Austin grad school buddies both scored 48, which is either great chemistry or proof that 48 is just what you get when you pick Antonelli and do everything else reasonably well, and the +5 BOX BOX pushed Drivetex past 100 for the first time this season. Moses Abdelshaid posted a strong 50 for XLIX but Krista Nabil's 32 left him without backup, and Drivetex (20 pts, 9th in Championship) gets on the board while XLIX (29 pts, 4th in Championship) drops one in a matchup where the Gold Rush ran out of gold.
