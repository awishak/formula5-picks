Miami Recap

Good Luck, Francisco

Before we get into this week's recap, I want to point out that Francisco Soldavini of Garra Dynamics will be racing for real in a BOSS GP event in Germany this weekend, racing cars from the Formula 2 class. Now, you might think this is where I would wish him good luck and leave it at that, but I am his professor in a Communication and Sport class, and so I have some advice, partially based on my viewing of F1 the movie with Brad Pitt.

  1. Go very fast. Usually cars that win are ones that are going very fast. That's just my analysis from what I've seen.
  2. If you can't go fast, then run into other cars and get a penalty or something that causes a safety car, and then your teammate can be first. Again, this is what Brad Pitt did in the movie and it seemed to work out pretty well, and I don't think there are any rules against it, except basically all the rules.
  3. If you don't want to run into other cars because it's a stupid strategy, also illegal, also incredibly dangerous, then my recommendation would be to potentially just take a shortcut somewhere, and then at the end of the race when they're like "hey, you're on lap 49," say "no, I'm actually on 50," and then maybe they'll believe you.

In all seriousness, good luck Francisco.

Battle at the Top

Van City Corsa came into Miami at 3-0 and sitting on top of the Championship Division, but then they ran into the golden team from Sacramento, XLIX, and got beaten by XLIX points... well, XLVIII (48) points. Close enough. Krista Nabil and Moses Abdelshaid both picked Antonelli, both scored 42 base, both got the finishing order bonus, and with the +5 BOX BOX they put up 89. Zarifa and Yousef both picked George Russell as top driver, scored 12 from that slot instead of Antonelli's 25, and Van City's 41 is their lowest team total of the season by a wide margin.

The 48-point gap is the largest in F5 history. XLIX (54 pts, 1st in Championship) takes the top spot with authority. Van City (47 pts, 2nd in Championship) gets humbled for the first time, and the lesson is clear: when half the league picks Russell and Antonelli wins again, the Russell teams pay the price.

Grant Wong and the Near-Perfect Week

Our big winner for the week is Grant Wong, who had a near-perfect week for HomeworkTubes.Com. He nailed the needle exactly at 2.2, got the finishing order bonus, the best finish bonus, and the weekly bonus. If he'd picked Sainz instead of Gasly he'd have maxed out everything. 57 points and first place.

He wasn't the only one having a good time. Eight players got the finishing order bonus this week, up from exactly two in the first three rounds combined. Wong, McGlynn, Schertler, Abdelshaid, Hanna, Nabil, Chris Malek, and Evie Ishak all nailed the order of their five drivers. Three DNFs from Lawson, Gasly, and Hadjar probably made the midfield order more predictable, but either way, the order bonus went from unicorn to real scoring category in one week. Five players also nailed the needle exactly at 2.2: Wong, Zarifa, Mudge, Fondacaro, and TJ Donato. I ran into Grant at the Los Gatos JV baseball game the Friday before the race, and I'm not saying I bring good luck to people in this league, but the data keeps piling up.

Last week, TJ Donato went to Suzuka and finished 7th. This week, Aditya Satish attended the Miami Grand Prix and finished 5th with 49 points, and Wildcat demolished Juicero 77-47. Two for two. Book your flights.
