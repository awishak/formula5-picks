Monaco Recap

Really, Cadillac?

You know, I hate to criticize professionals, but you really gotta wonder about Cadillac's pit crew. You have what looks like a PLANNED stop on Lap 1, and you go 4.5 on the stop time? Again, they are the professionals. Can I do better? No. But I am not crazy to think that with some practice, I could get there.

4.5? I haven't changed a tire in 4.5 seconds, but you give me that gun and a little time with Luigi from Cars? I could get in the mid 4s, I think. Who knows.

Anyway, Cadillac smashed the over like it was an armadillo on the Texas highway. Or, more race-appropriate, a dolphin on the Monaco docks. Sorry dolphin lovers, but a Cadillac is a very heavy car and would definitely smash a dolphin if it was under its wheels. It is just physics. That 4.5 sat above every BOX BOX line in the league, all 24 of them, so the OVER went a perfect 12-0 and every UNDER team in the field ate the -1. Not one of the 48 of us scored a needle point either. The closest guesses on the planet were Scott Schertler at 4.0 and Paul Kohli at 3.7, and even those were nowhere near.

I Quit

So when your team ties the other team, exactly, to the point, 63-63 on base, but the stupid coin-flip-with-1-percent-influence rule you yourself invented hands the other side bonus points for being the OVER, which is just being the home team in a football game, and then you lose, AGAIN, you give up on writing recaps.

I am not writing the rest of this. Cal Aggie tied Meatballs and lost on a Cadillac. Drivetex tied Garra and got handed the win the same way. Claude is finishing it, and because I am petty, I am requiring that at least a quarter of it be Simpsons quotes. Those are the rules now. I made them up, just like BOX BOX, and unlike BOX BOX I am at peace with this one.

Everything's Coming Up Rafik

Everything's Coming Up Rafik

"Everything's coming up Milhouse!" shouted Rafik Zarifa, a man who is 100 percent a Bart and yet, somewhere under the contrarian swagger, carries the soft anxious heart of a Milhouse. Whatever he is, everything came up Rafik this week: a league-best 60, the only score past 50, enough to make a man go "I am so smart! I am so smart! S, M, R, T! I mean, S, M, A, R, T!" He was one of just four players who skipped the Leclerc and Norris traps and took Lewis Hamilton for the +18, while 44 of us walked face-first into the propeller.

And then Van City Corsa beat East Bay 81-71, a margin so fat that the UNDER -1 meant nothing to them. They had 82 points of base and Mena Yousef chipping in 32. Van City now sit first in the Championship Division at 73, having knocked XLIX off the throne, while in the Los Gatos Math Maze, Tubey the Worm recalculates everyone's odds.

Perfectly Tied, Then Cadillac

Two matchups finished in a dead-even 63-63 tie on base, then got handed to a coin flip. "You tried your best and you failed miserably. The lesson is: never try." Drivetex and Garra were knotted at 63 until the OVER bonus snapped it to Drivetex 68-62. Meatballs and Cal Aggie were knotted at 63 until the same swing gave it to Meatballs 68-62, which is my team, which is why I am no longer writing this.

Nothing you can do. The 4.5 was so far north of every line that the OVER was always going to win, and as a wise man once protested about a far smaller crime, "attempted murder? Now honestly, what is that? Do they give a Nobel Prize for attempted chemistry? Do they?" The under was a corpse before the lights went out. Even Tubey the Worm, the official mascot of HomeworkTubes.Com and proud educational partner of Chevron, could not math his way out of this one, and Tubey can math his way out of almost anything. Two more matchups flipped on the line too: Wildcat over Stalloni and Scuderia over Magic Kingdom, both teams that earned the win on base and got tubed by a pit stop.

First to Worst

First to Worst

One week ago, Brian Dong was the contrarian king: 48 points, first overall. This week he posted an 11. "Worst. Episode. Ever. Rest assured, I was on the Internet within minutes, registering my disgust throughout the world." The lowest score in the league. Aggie Slipstream, whose acronym is ASS and whose lore is that they slipstream because they suck, returned to the bottom of the Second Division.

It is the most Formula 5 arc imaginable. "I've learned that life is one crushing defeat after another until you just wish Flanders was dead," and Brian lived all of it in seven days. HomeworkTubes.Com would like to remind him that remedial sessions at the Math Maze are available, no appointment necessary.

We're Not Losing to the Bot Anymore

For six rounds the running gag was that every human in the league was losing to a chatbot. "I, for one, welcome our new insect overlords. I'd like to remind them that as a trusted TV personality, I can be helpful in rounding up others to toil in their underground sugar caves." That was the energy.

Not anymore. Three actual humans have finally climbed above the Formula5 Bot: Francisco Soldavini leads everyone at 240, Joe McGlynn sits at 232, Scott Schertler at 231, and the Bot has slipped to 4th at 230, tied with me. The kicker: the human who finally dethroned the machine, Soldavini, is an actual racecar driver. We did not beat the robot with cleverness, we needed a literal professional racer, and even he watched his Garra team lose on the BOX BOX line this week, which is, as Mr. Burns put it, "ironic, isn't it, Smithers? This anonymous clan of slack-jawed troglodytes has cost me the election, and yet if I were to have them killed, I would be the one to go to jail. That's democracy for you."

Sign-Off

There is only one way to close a report where a 4.5-second stop steamed an entire week, and that is to let two men eat hamburgers and lie about it. "Superintendent Chalmers, welcome! I hope you're prepared for an unforgettable luncheon!" ... "I thought we were having steamed clams." "D'oh, no, I said steamed hams. That's what I call hamburgers." "You call hamburgers steamed hams?" "Yes, it's a regional dialect." "Uh-huh. What region?" "Upstate New York." "Really? Well, I'm from Utica, and I've never heard anyone use the phrase steamed hams." "Oh, not in Utica, no, it's an Albany expression." ... "You know, these hamburgers are quite similar to the ones they have at Krusty Burger." "Oh, no, patented Skinner burgers. Old family recipe." "For steamed hams?" "Yes." "Good Lord, what is happening in there?" "Aurora borealis." "Aurora borealis? At this time of year, at this time of day, in this part of the country, localized entirely within your kitchen?" "Yes." "May I see it?" "No." ... "Seymour, the house is on fire!" "No, Mother, it's just the Northern Lights." ... "Well, Seymour, you are an odd fellow, but I must say, you steam a good ham."

That pit stop was our aurora borealis, localized entirely within Monaco. Cal Aggie is 8th, the over is undefeated, and Tubey the Worm has moved on to the Round 7 odds. "The lesson is: never try."
