Really, Cadillac?

Round 6 -- Monaco Grand Prix
Monaco Recap

Really, Cadillac?

You know, I hate to criticize professionals, but you really gotta wonder about Cadillac's pit crew. You have what looks like a PLANNED stop on Lap 1, and you go 4.5 on the stop time?

Again, they are the professionals. Can I do better? No. But I am not crazy to think that with some practice, I could get there.

4.5? I haven't changed a tire in 4.5 seconds, but you give me that gun and a little time with Luigi from Cars? I could get in the mid 4s, I think. Who knows.

Anyway, Cadillac smashed the over like it was an armadillo on the Texas highway. Or, more race-appropriate, a dolphin on the Monaco docks. Sorry dolphin lovers, but a Cadillac is a very heavy car and would definitely smash a dolphin if it was under its wheels. It is just physics. That 4.5 sat above every BOX BOX line in the entire league, all 24 of them, so the OVER went a perfect 12-0 and every UNDER team in the field ate the -1.

I Quit (A Commissioner's Breakdown)

So when your team ties the other team, exactly, to the point, 63-63 on base, but the stupid coin-flip-with-1-percent-influence rule you yourself created hands the other team bonus points for being the OVER, which is basically being the home team in a football game, and then you lose, AGAIN, well. You give up on writing recaps.

I am not writing the rest of this recap. Cal Aggie tied Meatballs and lost on a Cadillac. Drivetex tied Garra and got handed the win the same way. I am done. Claude is finishing this. And because I am petty, the rest of this report is legally required to be at least one-quarter Simpsons quotes. Those are the rules now. I made them up, just like BOX BOX, and unlike BOX BOX I am at peace with this one.

Commissioner has left the building. The remainder of this report is brought to you by The Simpsons.
The Rest of It (Simpsons Mode)

Everything's Coming Up Rafik

"Everything's coming up Milhouse!" — Milhouse Van Houten Swap in Rafik and you have Rafik Zarifa's week. He posted a league-best 60, was one of only four players to pick Lewis Hamilton (+18), and stacked a field-high 32 midfield points on top. He took the BOX BOX -1 like everyone else who guessed under, and 60 points does not care.

It carried Van City Corsa to 81 in a romp over East Bay and shoved them to the top of the Championship Division at 73 points, knocking XLIX off the perch they had held all season. As Homer would put it: "I am so smart! I am so smart! S, M, R, T! I mean, S, M, A, R, T!" — Homer Simpson And on the win itself: "Marge, I'm going to be honest with you. We Stopped Losing To The Bot

A historic milestone in human-machine relations. As Kent Brockman once prepared us: "I, for one, welcome our new insect overlords. I'd like to remind them that as a trusted TV personality, I can be helpful in rounding up others to toil in their underground sugar caves." — Kent Brockman

But not today. Three actual humans have finally climbed above the Formula5 Bot in the cumulative standings: Francisco Soldavini leads at 240, Joe McGlynn sits at 232, and Scott Schertler at 231. The Bot slipped to 4th at 230, tied with me. The cruel part: the top two humans both lost on the BOX BOX line this week, which brings us to the rule I invented and now resent. "Ironic, isn't it, Smithers? This anonymous clan of slack-jawed troglodytes has cost me the election, and yet if I were to have them killed, I would be the one to go to jail. That's democracy for you." — Mr. Burns Replace "election" with "two matchups" and that is BOX BOX.
