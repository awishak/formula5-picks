Round 5 -- Canadian Grand Prix
Montreal Recap

Brian Flips the Slipstream Script

Brian Dong has been on a journey this season. Contrarian hero in Round 2 (the only player who skipped Norris). Contrarian casualty in Rounds 3 and 4. And now, in Round 5, the contrarian king is back on top with 48 points and the weekly win, his second career win after Austin last year. He was one of only three players in the entire league who picked Charles Leclerc as top driver, and when Russell and Norris both DNF'd in Montreal, that Leclerc pick turned into 12 points while the other 45 of us got -1.

And he didn't do it alone. His teammate Dan Patry also picked Leclerc (the third of only three Leclerc pickers in the whole league, and two of them are on Aggie), scored 38, and together they put up 66 to beat HomeworkTubes.Com 66-57. Aggie Slipstream finally has a W after going 0-4. And get this: Cal Aggie Racing and Aggie Slipstream are now tied at 30 pts in the Second Division standings. The commissioner's team and the team whose acronym is ASS are sitting right next to each other. I don't want to talk about it.

One Hundredth of a Second

Somewhere in Montreal, Audi mechanics tightened a wheel nut and sent a car on its way in 2.74 seconds. They had no idea that one hundredth of a second later, at 2.75, and the entire Prestissimo Veloce vs Magic Kingdom Racing matchup goes the other way. That's the closest we've been to a BOX BOX line, and actually, the closest we could be! What a fun matchup.

BOX BOX split 6-6 this week, the first time it wasn't a clean sweep in either direction. The Drivetex/East Bay and Peloton/Meatballs matchups were both flipped by the line, and the XLIX/Juicero matchup at 2.60 was also close.

Soldavini Leads the Human Division

Francisco Soldavini guessed 2.7 on the needle with the actual coming in at 2.74, close enough for maximum points. He scored 45 total (3rd this week) despite getting -1 on his Russell pick, because his 33 midfield points (tied with Harold Gutmann for best in the league) made up for it and then some. He's now the leader of the "human" division at 209 cumulative, with the Bot still in first at 214. After that, McGlynn, Stacy Michaelsen, and Fondacaro are all tied at 199, and I'm at 196.

We are all, once again, losing to the Bot.

45 Out of 48 Got Negative One

Antonelli won again (of course), but he wasn't in the top driver pool this week. The options were Russell, Leclerc, and Norris. Russell and Norris both DNF'd. That means 33 Russell pickers and 12 Norris pickers all got -1 on their top pick. Only the three Leclerc pickers (Dong, Dan Patry, Martin Nobar) got positive points from that slot.

The real separator was the midfield. Players who picked Franco Colapinto (P6, 8 pts) averaged 26.6 midfield points, while those who skipped him averaged 20.7. That 6-point gap was enormous. Lawson (P7, 6 pts) also helped, and the players who found the Colapinto/Lawson combo posted the highest midfield scores in the league.

Good Luck to Maddie Kohli Tonight!

Paul Kohli and El Camino Rapido lost a close one to Garra Dynamics, 59-61. Paul scored 22 points, which wouldn't be that notable except that his son Ryan Kohli on Garra scored 33 and helped beat him. Maybe Paul should have been as rapido as his daughter Maddie, who is running in the California High School State Championship tonight in the 4x400 and the 400. Or maybe the Rapido gene just skipped a generation. Good luck Maddie.

Luxor Motorsport 51, TJ Premium 42
Second Division
