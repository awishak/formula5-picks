Moving Day

Round 11 · Hungaroring · The first half ends

Budapest is two towns that eventually stopped arguing, Buda up on its hills and Pest laid flat across the water, the Danube between them doing what rivers always do, which is to look like a border while quietly functioning as a road.

Out past the suburbs, Lando Norris led from the front and Lewis Hamilton, who sat on more cards in this league than any other driver, went quietly backwards to fifth. Call the Grand Prix what you like. For the forty-eight people playing this thing it was only ever the afternoon that decided which bank they would wake up on.

The top four in the Second Division go up. The bottom four in the Championship Division go down. And one more seat changes hands on top, where whoever finishes fifth in the Second Division takes the eighth-placed Championship Division team's place if they scored better on average across the half.

First questionWhat if we just went by scoring average?

The table pays for winning your matchup and for how big that win looked next to everyone else's. It does not pay for scoring. So the two need not agree, and they do not.

PromotionCal Aggie Racing overcomes cost cap punishment, earns promotion

Cal Aggie were sent down for a cost cap violation, which in this league means Andrew Ishak signed the last two individual champions and the league decided that was quite enough of that. They have spent the half in the Second Division serving the sentence.

Cal Aggie needed to beat Peloton Aubergine to give themselves a strong chance of promotion.

The fixture list had put the only two realistic contenders for the final automatic place directly across from one another. They won it 87 to 75. Ishak and Kevin Coolidge submitted identical cards and scored identically, to the point. Stacy Michaelsen, playing against them, picked the same five drivers as both, added a bonus neither managed, and finished the highest scorer on the pitch. She lost because Brett Dillon, a Frenchman with a cyclist's vocabulary and a weakness for the purple vegetable his countrymen get romantic about, was the only one of the four to trust his own eye.

RelegationHow Cascadia did it

Rafik Zarifa built Van City Corsa in 2024 with the Nobar brothers. The brothers left, took the name of the whole rain-soaked bioregion instead of the one city, and became Cascadia Motorsport. They arrived in Hungary tenth of twelve and needing a result nobody expected.

Cascadia needed to beat Van City, and beat them convincingly, or go down.

Van City were top of the Championship Division and needed only a competent afternoon to finish the half there. Cascadia won 107 to 83, the biggest score anybody posted all round. Ronnie Nobar had the highest individual card of the weekend with all five of his drivers finishing in the exact order he had called, which comes off on fewer than one card in ten, and Martin gave him the rest.

Because championship points pay by rank among that week's winners, the biggest win took the maximum twenty-five. Cascadia went from tenth to seventh, out of the zone entirely, and the team they had broken away from lost the division on the same afternoon.

Second questionWhat if El Camino had scored four more?

Harold Gutmann and Paul Kohli went to second-placed Juicero Silicon Valley on the final day and beat them. They finished ninth, one point behind Garra, and were relegated by that single point. Their 86 was the fifth best win of the round and paid 10. Ninety would have paid 12.

The titleSacramento, from third, without ever leading

XLIX needed to beat Cougar Autosport and needed both teams above them to slip.

Krista Nabil and Moses Abdelshaid handled the first part 93 to 53, then stood still while the second part happened around them. There is gold in the American River and a bridge over the Sacramento whose towers spell out the number if you look at it right, and the team that carries both takes the first half having led it at no point whatsoever.

They also gambled harder on the pit stop than almost anybody.

  • Garra needed only to beat Bronco and were safe. They lost, fell to eighth, and the swap took the seat.
  • Bronco needed a big win and three other results. They got the win, exactly as big as the maths demanded, and not one of the three arrived.
  • Stalloni needed to beat Drivetex and were beaten instead.

The second halfWhich bank you woke up on

Now everybody gets a break, which is the other thing that happens at the end of term.

Buda

The twelve on the Buda side have a hill in front of them. The castle sits on top and only one of them reaches it, which is another way of saying eleven teams will spend the entire second half climbing and not arriving. There is a funicular. It runs up from the Buda end of the chain bridge, it takes about ninety seconds, and Andrew Ishak wants to ride it more than he has ever wanted anything in his life. Nobody is getting on it. You go up on foot or you do not go up.

And over on the Pest side, flat and wide and a long way from any hill at all, the question for the next eleven rounds is simply how close to the water you can get. Finish near enough to the bank and the Széchenyi is right there, lions at either end, the whole thing lit up over the black river, and you cross in the spring and start climbing in 2027.

Heraclitus had the line about never stepping in the same river twice, and he was right about the water and wrong about everything else, because the banks stay exactly where they have always been. It is only ever the people who move.
