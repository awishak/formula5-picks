Moving Day

Round 11 · Hungaroring · The first half ends

Budapest is two towns that eventually stopped arguing, Buda up on its hills and Pest laid flat across the water, the Danube between them doing what rivers always do, which is to look like a border while quietly functioning as a road.

Out past the suburbs, Lando Norris led from the front and Lewis Hamilton, who sat on more cards in this league than any other driver, went quietly backwards to fifth. Call the Grand Prix what you like. For the forty-eight people playing this thing it was only ever the afternoon that decided which bank they would wake up on.

The top four in the Second Division go up. The bottom four in the Championship Division go down. And one more seat changes hands on top of that, where whoever finishes fifth in the Second Division takes the eighth-placed Championship Division team's place if they scored better on average across the half.

PromotionThe team that overspent, coming home

Cal Aggie Racing were sent down for a cost cap violation, which in this league means Andrew Ishak signed the last two individual champions and the league decided that was quite enough of that.

Cal Aggie needed to beat Peloton Aubergine to give themselves a strong chance of promotion.

The fixture list had put the only two realistic contenders for that final automatic place directly across from one another, which is a tidiness sport almost never provides. They won it 87 to 75. Ishak and Kevin Coolidge submitted identical cards and scored identically, to the point. Stacy Michaelsen, playing against them, had picked the same five drivers as both, added a bonus neither managed, and finished the highest scorer on the pitch. She lost because Brett Dillon, a Frenchman with a cyclist's vocabulary and a weakness for the purple vegetable his countrymen get romantic about, was the only one of the four to trust his own eye.

Peloton needed either to win, or to lose while having scored well enough across the half to claim the swap seat.

They lost. They had scored well enough. Both teams from that match go up, and above them Meatballs, HomeworkTubes.Com and TNT Roku had settled the automatic places some time earlier. Which leaves the parent club: Cal Aggie exist because Ishak broke them off from Aggie Slipstream in 2025, and Aggie Slipstream finished eleventh, a team whose entire recorded history is a three-letter acronym and the frank admission that they draft behind people because they are not quick enough to lead.

RelegationThe sons came back across the water

Rafik Zarifa built Van City Corsa in 2024 with the Nobar brothers, borrowing the name from the city his wife's family comes from, where the great local contribution to human happiness is a Nanaimo bar and nobody has ever needed to turn an oven on to make one. Then the brothers left, traded the one city for the whole rain-soaked bioregion around it, and became Cascadia Motorsport.

They arrived tenth of twelve, given about a one in five chance of surviving. LeBron's Cavaliers were given worse against a side that had won seventy-three games, and there is a reason nobody remembers the four series before it.

Cascadia needed to beat Van City, and beat them convincingly, or go down.

They won 107 to 83. Ronnie Nobar posted the highest individual score of the round with all five of his drivers finishing in the exact order he had called, which comes off on fewer than one card in ten. They took the maximum available and left the relegation zone without ever needing to look at another scoreboard. Van City had come needing only a competent afternoon to finish the half on top. What turned up instead was the team they founded, arriving from underneath and taking everything on the table.

RelegationWhat Santa Clara lost in an afternoon

There is an arithmetic anybody who has taught long enough learns to live with, which is that the room refills every September regardless of what happened in it last year. Professor Ishak has been doing this at Santa Clara since 2012, long enough that the woman who won the Second Division on Sunday was in the very first class he ever taught there.

His university had four teams in the top flight. It has none now. There was the mascot smuggled into a word Ferrari use for a stable, and the year the doors first opened carried on a badge, and the long fast road the campus has always sat beside, and two freshmen who went looking for a Spanish word that means claw and grit and the willingness to keep fighting, and found one.

Garra needed only to beat Bronco and they were safe, full stop.

They were heavy favourites, the one team in the drop zone whose fate belonged entirely to them, and Alicia Cho produced the round's other perfect finishing order to beat them 89 to 68. Garra fell to eighth, the swap came for the eighth-placed team, and the side that had gone looking for a word about fighting went down without ever having been out of its own control until the moment it lost.

El Camino needed a win, and produced the best result of their short life.

Harold Gutmann, who works on that campus, and Paul Kohli, who graduated from it, went to second-placed Juicero Silicon Valley on the final day and beat them. They finished ninth, one point behind Garra, and were relegated by that single point.

Two Kohlis were in that relegation fight, one a freshman and one an alum, and the river took them both. One lost the match he could not lose. The other won a match nobody thought he could. Same bank, come morning.

The titleSacramento, from third, without ever leading

XLIX needed to beat Cougar Autosport and needed both teams above them to slip.

Krista Nabil and Moses Abdelshaid handled the first part 93 to 53, the most lopsided result of the round, then stood still while the second part happened around them. There is gold in the American River and a bridge over the Sacramento whose towers spell out the number if you look at it right, and the team that carries both takes the first half of the Championship Division having led it at no point whatsoever.

The mechanicThe season the pit stop rewrote

The BOX BOX line is a side bet on one pit stop. Get the side right and the team takes five points into the matchup. Get it wrong and lose one. It sounds like a garnish.

Everything elseThe rest of the half, briefly

  • Bronco needed a big win and three other results to fall their way. They got the win, exactly as big as the maths demanded, and not one of the three arrived.
  • Stalloni needed to beat Drivetex and were beaten instead.
  • Luxor won nine matches, more than anyone in either division, and were not promoted. Without the pit stop line they would have been.
  • Meatballs won the Second Division while being taken apart 97 to 62 by Prestissimo Veloce, the professor's daughter Evie and her friend Matilda, who had nothing to play for.
  • HomeworkTubes did every single thing available, posted the best winning score in the division, and finished one point short of a total that had been unreachable before the lights went out.
  • Scuderia Iskandaraya beat Aggie Slipstream 69 to 57 and finished last anyway. Aggie Slipstream avoided the wooden spoon by losing.
  • Wildcat lost to East Bay and went down with them.

The second halfWhich bank you woke up on

Now everybody gets a break, which is the other thing that happens at the end of term.

Buda

The twelve on the Buda side have a hill in front of them. The castle sits on top and only one of them reaches it, which is another way of saying eleven teams will spend the entire second half climbing and not arriving. There is a funicular. It runs up from the Buda end of the chain bridge, it takes about ninety seconds, and Andrew Ishak wants to ride it more than he has ever wanted anything in his life. Nobody is getting on it. You go up on foot or you do not go up.

And over on the Pest side, flat and wide and a long way from any hill at all, the question for the next eleven rounds is simply how close to the water you can get. Finish near enough to the bank and the Széchenyi is right there, lions at either end, the whole thing lit up over the black river, and you cross in the spring and start climbing in 2027.

Heraclitus had the line about never stepping in the same river twice, and he was right about the water and wrong about everything else, because the banks stay exactly where they have always been. It is only ever the people who move.
