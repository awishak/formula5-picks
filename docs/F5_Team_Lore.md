# Formula 5 Team Lore and Origins (2026 Season)

All 24 teams, their players, and the stories behind the names.

## Championship Division

### 1. Van City Corsa
Players: R. Zarifa and M. Yousef
Rafik married Mena's sister, who is from Vancouver, hence "Van City." Rafik co-founded the team with the Cascadia guys in 2024. Before that, Rafik was on East Bay Racing. "Corsa" is Italian for race.

### 2. El Camino Rapido
Players: H. Gutmann and P. Kohli
New team. Gutmann works at SCU, which sits on El Camino Real, "The Fast Road" in Spanish. Paul Kohli is an SCU alum.

### 3. Juicero Silicon Valley
Players: G. Fahmy and K. Nakhla
Original team since 2023. George is a founding member. Both players are from the Bay Area. Named after the infamous Silicon Valley juicer startup.

### 4. XLIX Racing Team
Players: K. Nabil and M. Abdelshaid
XLIX = 49, a reference to the 1849 Gold Rush and Sacramento. The logo is the Tower Bridge designed to also read as XLIX.

### 5. Wildcat Motors
Players: A. Satish and T. Ishak
Original team. Both players are Los Gatos High School classmates, the Los Gatos Wildcats.

### 6. Bronco SCUderia
Players: A. Cho and M. Mudge
Original team. Two former students of Professor Ishak at Santa Clara University. "SCU" is embedded inside "Scuderia" (Ferrari's team prefix), and SCU's mascot is the Broncos.

### 7. Cougar Autosport
Players: H. Ishak and L. Thompson
Original team. An adult Los Gatos community team (not a university mascot reference).

### 8. Cascadia Motorsport
Players: M. Nobar and R. Nobar
Was originally part of Van City Corsa. Cascadia refers to the Pacific Northwest bioregion.

### 9. Stalloni 1851
Players: J. Civitts and S. Schertler
Founded in 2025 by a group of former SCU men, inspired by Jack Civitts' move to Cal Poly at that time. "Stalloni" = stallions in Italian. 1851 is SCU's founding year.

### 10. East Bay Racing
Players: J. Hanna and Z. Girgis
Original team. Friends from the East Bay. Peep the logo.

### 11. Drivetex
Players: N. Brody and J. McGlynn
Original team. Two friends from grad school at UT Austin. One grew up in Texas, one lives there now. Drive + Texas.

### 12. Garra Dynamics
Players: R. Kohli and F. Soldavini
New team. Two SCU freshmen, one from Argentina. "Garra" means claw (or grit/fight) in Spanish.

## Second Division

### 1. TNT Roku F5 Team
Players: A. Thompson and M. Thompson
TNT = **T**hompson **N** **T**hompson. Andy works at Roku.

### 2. Shoey Time! w/ Max and Danny
Players: M. Reisinger and D. Bowers
Two former students of Professor Ishak from the same class, Fall 2022 Public Speaking. Named after Daniel Ricciardo's shoey celebration. "Max and Danny" mirrors the old Verstappen/Ricciardo Red Bull pairing.

### 3. Meatballs
Players: A. Carnesecca and M. Ball
Two former students of Professor Ishak. **Carne**secca (meat) + **Ball** = Meatball. Maggie was in Professor Ishak's very first class at SCU in Fall 2012.

### 4. Cal Aggie Racing
Players: A. Ishak and K. Coolidge
Spinoff of Aggie Slipstream. Founded in 2025 by Andrew Ishak. UC Davis Aggies. Relegated to Second Division for 2026 after a cost cap violation (signed the last two individual champions).

### 5. Scuderia Iskandaraya
Players: A. Zamary and R. Stephanos
Two Coptic guys, friends from church. "Iskandaraya" is the Arabic name for Alexandria, Egypt.

### 6. Luxor Motorsport
Players: P. Attalah and C. Malek
Same Coptic community connection, close friends from Hayward Church. Named after the ancient Egyptian city of Luxor.

### 7. Peloton Aubergine
Players: B. Dillon and S. Michaelsen
Brett is into biking and is French. "Aubergine" is the French word for eggplant. Stacy loves her Peloton bike and eggplant. A beautifully absurd team name.

### 8. Prestissimo Veloce
Players: E. Ishak and M. Luton
Evie (Professor Ishak's daughter) and her classmate/friend Matilda. "Prestissimo" is a music tempo marking (very fast), a nod to Evie's love of singing. "Veloce" = fast in Italian.

### 9. TJ Premium
Players: T. Donato and F. Bot
Self-explanatory. TJ is T. Donato. F. Bot is the Formula5 Bot (Claude AI). The name is deliberately underwhelming.

### 10. Magic Kingdom Racing
Players: C. Fondacaro and L. Noel
Big Disney fans. Larry lives in Florida (near the actual Magic Kingdom), Chris is in SoCal (near Disneyland). Both are Professor Ishak's friends from elementary school.

### 11. HomeworkTubes.Com
Players: S. Bottoms and G. Wong
HomeworkTubes.com is a real website. Both players were formerly on Cougar Autosport together. All three (including Professor Ishak) coached little league together.

### 12. Aggie Slipstream
Players: B. Dong and D. Patry
Original team. The acronym is ASS. They slipstream because they suck. That's it. That's the lore.

## Meta Notes

- The league draws heavily from Professor Ishak's overlapping communities: SCU (students past and present), the Bay Area Coptic Egyptian community, UC Davis alumni, Los Gatos neighbors, and childhood friends.
- Italian racing naming conventions (Scuderia, Corsa, Stalloni, Veloce) are a recurring motif.
- Egyptian cultural pride runs through several teams (Iskandaraya, Luxor, XLIX's Sacramento roots).
- Several teams have portmanteau or hidden-word names (Bronco **SCU**deria, **Carne**secca + **Ball**, **A**ggie **S**lip**s**tream).
